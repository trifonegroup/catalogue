## Shared DNA Across All Three References

These three electronics/gadget e-commerce sites share a tight visual language:

### Layout & Structure
- Full-width hero banner with large product photography and a short headline + CTA button
- Horizontal scrolling product carousels (5–6 cards wide on desktop)
- Section titles right/center aligned with "View All" link on the opposite side
- Category icon strip — icon above label, 4–6 categories in a row
- Brand logo marquee row (greyscale or full-color logos on white)
- Two-column promotional banner row (one dark, one colorful) between product sections
- Dark footer with 3–4 columns: quick links, contact, newsletter

### Color
- **Primary action color:** Blue — `#0066CC` range (buttons, badges, highlights)
- **Danger/sale badge:** Red-orange — `#E63946` or `#FF4B2B`
- **Background:** White `#FFFFFF` for cards; very light gray `#F5F6F8` for page bg
- **Header:** Dark navy `#0A0F2C`–`#1A1F3C` or near-black `#111827`
- **Footer:** Dark navy matching header
- **Accent/promo banners:** Teal, purple, or yellow used sparingly for contrast

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, heavy weight (700–800), large scale — product names punchy and short
- **Body/prices:** Medium weight, clean sans-serif
- **Persian reference (NOVA):** RTL layout, same hierarchy rules apply
- Price styling: current price prominent, original price smaller + strikethrough, discount % in red badge

### Cards
- White card, 8–12px border radius
- Subtle box shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`)
- Product image centered with padding, white background
- Discount badge: top-left corner, red pill/square
- "New" badge: top-left, green
- Quick-action icons (wishlist, compare, cart) appear on hover
- Star rating row below product name in orange/gold (`#FFA500`)
- Two prices stacked: sale price (bold, dark) + original (gray, strikethrough)

### Buttons
- Primary CTA: solid blue, rounded (4–6px radius), uppercase or title case, medium weight
- Secondary: outlined or ghost version of primary
- "Add to Cart": full-width inside card on hover, or persistent below price

### Promotional Banners
- Dark banner: near-black or navy bg, white headline, colored sub-text, outlined CTA button
- Colorful banner: teal/green or yellow bg, dark headline, solid CTA
- Banners use product cutout images floating to one side

### Navigation
- Top utility bar: phone number, free shipping text, language/currency toggle
- Main nav: logo left, search bar center (wide), icons right (wishlist, cart, account)
- Category nav bar below: horizontal list of department links

## Reference-Specific Notes

### Reference 1 — NOVA (Persian RTL electronics store)
- RTL layout — all alignment rules are mirrored
- Hero: deep blue-to-purple gradient with glowing product imagery
- Strong use of blue `#2563EB` for CTAs and section headers
- Light blue tinted card rows (`#EFF6FF` background for sections)
- Pagination dots below hero carousel

### Reference 2 — eCommax (WooCommerce theme)
- Charcoal header (`#1C1C2E`) with purple "All Departments" pill
- Blue `#0099FF` for primary buttons and links
- Countdown timers on "Deals of the Day" cards (dark digit blocks)
- Lifestyle photography in category cards (not just icons)
- Dark green used for brand showcase background — not to replicate, just note

### Reference 3 — Elehaus
- Red-orange `#E84545` for "All Departments" dropdown — more aggressive accent
- Left sidebar filter panel on product listing pages
- Yellow `#FFD700` full-width promotional banner — high contrast
- Colored promo banners: deep blue + black pairing
- Smaller, denser card grid than the other two references
- Blog section at bottom with date + view count metadata

## What to Prioritize
1. Clean white cards with subtle shadow — no heavy borders
2. Dark navy header and footer bookending light body
3. Blue as the primary trust/action color
4. Red badges for discounts — always visible, never subtle
5. Rich product imagery — let products dominate cards
6. Consistent spacing rhythm — generous padding inside sections
